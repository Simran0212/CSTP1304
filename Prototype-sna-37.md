Journey Prototype by Simran

Prototype Details
--------------------------
In this application, Journey, users are able to receive a diet, exercise, or sleep plan based on the final result they receive after answering some questions. The app is made using HTML, JavaScript, BootStrap, and CSS.

Live ProtoType:


Scenario 1
------------
A second year college student is struggling to get proper sleep. They feel tired in school after sleeping at 1:00AM and waking up at 6:30AM. They spend their time on assignments at night before sleeping. The app, Journey, provides them with a plan that they can follow to help improve their sleep schedule and quality. Journey gives them a list to follow below: 

- Sleep 7-9 hours per night

- Go to bed before 11 PM

- Avoid screens 1 hour before bed

- Reduce caffeine after 2 PM

- Create a consistent sleep schedule


Scenario 2
------------
A user wants to lose weight but is struggling to figure out how to incorporate exercises into their lifestyle. They do not know where to start. The app, Journey gives them a list to follow below: 

- Walk 30 minutes daily

- 8,000-10,000 steps per day

- Cardio 3 times weekly

- Strength training twice weekly

- Track progress each week

Scenario 3
------------
A user wants to eat healthier after noticing that they always feel bloated and have low energy levels after eating out. The user was told to cut back on eating out, but they do not know where to start. The app, Journey gives them a list to follow below: 

- Eat breakfast daily

- Drink 8 glasses of water

- Reduce fast food consumption

- Include vegetables in every meal

- Follow a balanced meal schedule



Colour Scheme 
------
- For the background, the colour is white. Color: #f5f5f5, 
RGB: rgb(245, 245, 245). The reason I chose this colour as many websites have white backgrounds.
- For the navigation bar, the color is blue. Color: #0187f5
RGB: rgb(1, 135, 245). The reason I chose this colour because blue is known to be used in health apps to show trust
- For the hero section below the navigation bar, the color is green. Color: #009619
RGB: rgb(0, 150, 25). The reason I chose this colour because green is known to represent health, wellness and positivity.
- For the text on the hero section, the colour is white. Color: #f5f5f5, 
RGB: rgb(245, 245, 245). The reason I chose this colour as it contrasts the bright green.
- For the footer, the colour is dark grey. Color: #333333
RGB: rgb(51, 51, 51). The reason I chose this colour as it contrasts the rest of the bright colours I picked above.



- The colour system used is: Functional UI Color System
- Green represent health, which aligns with the overall perspective of the application. While, blue represents trust, and white helps contrast the bright colours.
